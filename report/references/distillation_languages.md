# Distillation for language ability: verified references (collected 2026-11-19)

Checked by opening each arXiv abstract page on 2026-11-19 (WebFetch). Quotes verbatim.

**[Odumakinde2024]** Odumakinde, D'souza, Verga, Ermis, Hooker (2024). *Multilingual Arbitrage: Optimizing Data Pools to Accelerate Multilingual Progress.* arXiv:2408.14960 (preprint, no venue listed). https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14960
- Several teacher models generate multilingual training data for a student; "gains of up to 56.5% improvement in win rates averaged across all languages".
- Measures judged preference (win rates), i.e. chat quality, not knowledge.

**[Dang2024-AyaExpanse]** Dang et al. (45 authors) (2024). *Aya Expanse: Combining Research Breakthroughs for a New Multilingual Frontier.* arXiv:2412.04261 (preprint). https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04261
- Built with "advancements in data arbitrage, multilingual preference training, and model merging".
- Evaluated on "the Arena-Hard-Auto dataset, translated into 23 languages"; "Aya Expanse 32B outperforms Llama 3.1 70B, a model with twice as many parameters, achieving a 54.0% win-rate."
- Judged preference again; the abstract does not say "distillation" explicitly (data arbitrage = teacher-generated data).

**[GemmaTeam2024-Gemma2]** Gemma Team (2024). *Gemma 2: Improving Open Language Models at a Practical Size.* arXiv:2408.00118 (technical report, preprint). https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118
- "We also train the 2B and 9B models with knowledge distillation (Hinton et al., 2015) instead of next token prediction."
- The models "deliver the best performance for their size" and "offer competitive alternatives to models that are 2-3 times bigger."
- Distillation during PRE-training: the route with evidence of building general ability, at pre-training cost.

**[Li2023-BactrianX]** Li, Koto, Wu, Aji, Baldwin (2023). *Bactrian-X: Multilingual Replicable Instruction-Following Models with Low-Rank Adaptation.* arXiv:2305.15011 (preprint). https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15011
- "a comprehensive multilingual parallel dataset of 3.4 million instruction-response pairs across 52 languages"; LoRA models "outperform both the vanilla models and existing instruction-tuned models".
- The abstract page does not confirm the pairs were generated with ChatGPT.

**Counterpoint already verified:** Okapi (Lai et al., EMNLP 2023 demo; see post_training_language.md): instruction data generated by ChatGPT in many languages, then SFT/RLHF; LLaMA 7B MMLU 29.8 -> 30.1 -> 30.8. Teacher-written post-training data improves behaviour more than knowledge.

**Also relevant (post_training_language.md):** DeepSeek-R1 (Nature 2025): distilled DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B 72.6 on AIME 2024 vs 47.0 for RL alone on the same base (maths, not language).

## Reading for SE.LLMA
- Distillation as post-training (teacher-written Swedish answers) is cheap and improves Swedish chat quality and fluency; evidence it adds knowledge is weak.
- Distillation during pre-training (student learns from a teacher's output distribution over large amounts of text) is how strong small models such as Gemma 2 are built; it is the best-supported way to give a small Swedish model more ability, at pre-training cost. Not yet tested specifically for Swedish.
